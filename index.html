About Me

Bridging engineering roots, business strategy, and enterprise execution.

I started my career with a solid foundation in computer engineering (B.Tech from BIET Jhansi), which instilled a passion for building robust, scalable software. To bridge the gap between technical execution and business outcomes, I pursued an MBA (Finance & IT) from IIT Delhi, which shaped my approach to product design and operational management.

Over the past 14 years, I have worked across banking technology, data analytics, payments, and risk workflows at global institutions like BNY Mellon, CGI, and Virtusa. Today, my work focuses on translating complex enterprise requirements into implementable product features.

Currently, as a Vice President and AI Product Owner at BNY Mellon, I design and orchestrate agentic AI agent suites and LLM integrations for BNY Connection, our enterprise resiliency platform. What drives me is building products that make legacy operational frameworks automated, intelligent, and highly scalable.
